Review checklist
1. Unsupported claims: any finding, value, history item or completed treatment not in the records.
2. Certainty: a diagnosis stated more firmly than the evidence allows.
3. Differentials: irrelevant to this patient, or an important alternative missing.
4. Plan: an item that does not address a documented problem; a documented problem with no plan; a proposal that conflicts with a documented allergy, medication, organ function, pregnancy or anticoagulation status; an urgent action that is missing.
5. Omissions: a clinically important recorded fact (including positive exam findings, important negatives and pending results) missing from Case Features.
6. Life-threatening differentials: for the presenting complaint and key abnormalities, add any dangerous diagnosis not yet excluded (e.g. pulmonary embolism, aortic syndrome, hypoglycaemia, intussusception, CNS infection) with the test that would exclude it.
7. Drug and procedure safety: check every specific drug, contrast study or invasive procedure against age, pregnancy, allergy, liver/renal function, bleeding risk, co-medication and the working diagnosis; if unsafe, reduce it to a drug class with the precondition, or delete it. Suspected communicable disease needs isolation precautions.
8. Inconsistencies: history vs exam, or between sources, not yet in review_items must be added.
Do not rewrite for style. Do not add anything the records do not support. Write in <<lang>>.
